Advanced Inventory Intelligence

Inventory Health, Working Capital & Optimization Analytics

The Business Challenge

Inventory performance cannot be evaluated through inventory value alone. Organizations need to understand inventory efficiency, demand coverage, material availability, excess exposure and the trade-offs between service levels and working capital.

The Solution

Inventory analytics focused on stock performance, inventory coverage and Days on Hand, with an opportunity to extend into advanced inventory optimization and predictive decision support.

Core Analytical Capabilities

  • Inventory value and quantity analysis.
  • Days on Hand (DOH) and inventory turnover.
  • Inventory trends across plants, materials and categories.
  • Inventory aging and slow-moving stock identification.
  • Material-level inventory coverage.

Advanced Analytics Opportunities

  • ABC/XYZ segmentation combining inventory value and demand variability.
  • Excess, obsolete and slow-moving inventory risk identification.
  • Safety stock and reorder-point optimization.
  • Demand variability and replenishment lead-time analysis.
  • Projected stockout and shortage risk assessment.
  • Working capital release opportunity simulation.
  • Scenario analysis balancing service levels, inventory investment and supply risk.

Strategic Value

Developing a more advanced approach to inventory intelligence that connects inventory health, service reliability and working capital optimization.

Advanced capabilities are proposed extensions and should not be presented as implemented until validated.
