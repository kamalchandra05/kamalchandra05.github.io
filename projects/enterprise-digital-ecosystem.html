Enterprise Digital Transformation

Enterprise Digital Solutions & Analytics Ecosystem

Scalable Business Intelligence, Applications & Process Automation

The Business Challenge

Large organizations often develop isolated reports, applications and manual processes across functions. Without standardization, this creates inconsistent KPIs, duplicated development effort and fragmented decision-making.

The Solution

Led the development and scaling of an enterprise digital analytics ecosystem combining business intelligence, application development, process automation and standardized performance governance.

Portfolio Scale

  • 200+ Power BI dashboards delivered or supported.
  • 50+ digital applications and application-development initiatives, subject to final portfolio validation.
  • 45+ automated business processes.
  • 15+ standardized enterprise procurement KPIs.
  • Cross-functional solutions spanning procurement, supply chain, engineering, finance and business operations.

Key Capabilities

  • Executive, operational and analytical dashboards.
  • Power BI and Power Apps integrated solutions.
  • Workflow automation and process digitalization.
  • Enterprise KPI standardization.
  • Data governance and stewardship.
  • Self-service analytics and business enablement.
  • Scalable reporting and application frameworks.

Business Impact

Created a broader digital operating model that supports business teams through standardized information, streamlined processes and accessible analytical capabilities.

Transformation Philosophy

The objective is not simply to build more dashboards or applications, but to create reusable enterprise capabilities that improve how the organization operates and makes decisions.
