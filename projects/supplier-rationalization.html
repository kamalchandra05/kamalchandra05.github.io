Strategic Procurement Transformation

Supplier Rationalization Intelligence

Data-Driven Supplier Base Optimization

The Business Challenge

A large and fragmented supplier base increases procurement complexity, governance requirements and supplier management effort.

The Solution

Developed a data-driven supplier rationalization framework combining supplier segmentation, material-level analysis, sourcing intelligence and governance.

Key Capabilities

  • Direct and indirect supplier segmentation.
  • Supplier portfolio and spend analysis.
  • Material-level BOM analysis.
  • Alternate-source identification.
  • Make-versus-buy visibility.
  • Supplier consolidation opportunity identification.
  • Governance and rationalization tracking.

Business Impact

Supported the reduction of approximately 20,000 suppliers over an 18-month period through a structured, analytics-driven rationalization approach.
