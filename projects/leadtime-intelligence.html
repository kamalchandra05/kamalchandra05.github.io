Lead-Time Intelligence

Supplier Lead-Time Visibility & Performance Analytics

The Business Challenge

Inaccurate or outdated lead-time information can affect procurement planning, supplier delivery measurement and operational decision-making.

The Solution

Developed analytical capabilities to examine supplier lead-time data, identify inconsistencies and improve visibility into lead-time performance.

Key Capabilities

  • Supplier and material lead-time analysis.
  • Comparison of maintained and observed lead times.
  • Identification of lead-time data inconsistencies.
  • Supplier delivery performance correlation.
  • Exception reporting and corrective-action visibility.

Strategic Value

Improved transparency around lead-time maintenance and its relationship with supplier delivery performance.
