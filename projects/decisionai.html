DecisionAI

Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform

The Business Challenge

Business and functional teams often depend on multiple disconnected tools for reporting, analysis and decision support. Moving from a business question to an informed decision can require navigating several dashboards, combining data from different sources and relying on central analytics or technical teams.

The Solution

DecisionAI is an independently developed enterprise intelligence platform designed to bring business domains into a unified analytical workspace.

The platform aims to empower users, regardless of their technical background, to explore data, identify patterns, understand business performance, evaluate scenarios and make informed decisions.

Key Capabilities

  • Quick Insight Builder for guided analytics and reporting.
  • AI Assistant for natural-language exploration of business data.
  • Custom insight reports and collaborative analytical workspaces.
  • Intelligence 360° with a catalogue of 91 analytical engines across business functions.
  • Statistical, diagnostic, predictive and simulation-based analytical methods.
  • Multi-domain intelligence covering Procurement, Supply Chain, Finance, Sales & Commercial, Risk and Leadership.

Strategic Value

Moving business intelligence beyond centralized reporting toward accessible, self-service decision intelligence, with the objective of reducing analytical dependency and improving decision-making speed.

Independent development; capabilities are at varying stages of development and validation.
