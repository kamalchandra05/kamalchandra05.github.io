Procurement, Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Purchased Material vs. In-House Production Intelligence

Make-or-Buy & Manufacturing Value Chain Analytics

The Business Challenge

Organizations need visibility into how purchased materials contribute to internal production, where external supplier dependency exists and how manufacturing and sourcing strategies influence total value-chain performance.

The Solution

An analytical framework connecting purchased materials, production structures and internal manufacturing activity to provide a clearer understanding of external procurement and in-house production relationships.

Key Analytical Dimensions

  • Purchased material versus internally produced material classification.
  • External procurement and internal production mix.
  • Material and BOM relationship analysis.
  • Plant-level purchasing and production visibility.
  • Purchased-component dependency analysis.

Advanced Analytical Opportunities

  • Make-versus-buy scenario evaluation.
  • Supplier dependency and manufacturing concentration risk.
  • Internal capacity and outsourcing trade-offs.
  • Total cost of ownership comparisons.
  • Manufacturing value-add analysis.
  • Vertical integration opportunities.
  • Alternate sourcing and production scenario modeling.

Strategic Value

Supports a more integrated understanding of procurement, manufacturing and sourcing decisions across the enterprise value chain.

Advanced capabilities represent potential extensions requiring further validation.
