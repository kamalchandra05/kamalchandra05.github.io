New Product Introduction Intelligence

Engineering Project Governance through Power BI & Power Apps

The Business Challenge

New Product Introduction projects require coordination among engineering, procurement and management teams. Monitoring milestones through dashboards alone does not provide a structured mechanism for capturing feedback, escalating issues or tracking management actions.

The Solution

Developed an integrated digital solution combining Power BI and Power Apps to connect project performance monitoring with collaborative management inputs.

Key Capabilities

  • NPI KPI dashboards and project milestone tracking.
  • Engineering and procurement performance visibility.
  • Power Apps interfaces for management feedback.
  • Structured recording of project alerts and concerns.
  • Cross-functional input from engineering, procurement and management.
  • Improved visibility of project progress and areas requiring attention.

Strategic Value

Connected performance measurement with operational feedback, creating a more collaborative framework for NPI project governance and management oversight.
