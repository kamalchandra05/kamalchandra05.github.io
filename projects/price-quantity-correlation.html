Procurement & Commercial Analytics

Purchase Price & Invoice Quantity Correlation Intelligence

Advanced Material Price Behavior Analytics

The Business Challenge

Purchase price movements are influenced by multiple factors, including order quantities, supplier agreements, material characteristics, commodity conditions and purchasing behavior.

Understanding the relationship between invoice quantities and purchase prices can reveal opportunities that are not visible through conventional price variance reporting.

The Solution

Developed analytical approaches to examine purchase price and invoice quantity relationships across procurement categories, subcommodities and materials.

Key Analytical Dimensions

  • Price-versus-invoice-quantity correlation.
  • Category and subcommodity comparisons.
  • Material-level purchasing behavior.
  • Supplier-level price variation.
  • Volume-related price patterns.
  • Identification of unusual price and quantity relationships.

Advanced Analytical Opportunities

  • Regression-based price sensitivity analysis.
  • Nonlinear volume-price relationships.
  • Outlier and exception detection.
  • Commodity-adjusted price analysis.
  • Identification of potential volume consolidation opportunities.
  • Distinguishing correlation from actual volume-driven pricing effects.

Strategic Value

Enables deeper understanding of purchasing behavior and supports fact-based sourcing, negotiation and procurement optimization opportunities.
