U.S. Tariff Impact Intelligence

Rapid Enterprise Exposure & Scenario Analysis

The Business Challenge

Changes in U.S. tariff policies created an urgent need to understand potential cost exposure across materials, suppliers, countries and procurement categories.

Traditional reporting approaches could delay management's ability to evaluate the financial and operational implications of new trade measures.

The Solution

Developed a rapid analytical framework to identify tariff exposure, estimate incremental cost implications and support leadership evaluation of potential scenarios.

Key Capabilities

  • Country-of-origin and supplier exposure analysis.
  • Material and procurement-category-level impact visibility.
  • Incremental tariff cost estimation.
  • Identification of potentially affected procurement spend.
  • Scenario-based decision support for leadership.
  • Prioritization of areas requiring commercial or sourcing review.

Business Impact

Enabled rapid executive visibility into emerging tariff risks, supporting timely assessment of potential cost exposure and mitigation priorities.
